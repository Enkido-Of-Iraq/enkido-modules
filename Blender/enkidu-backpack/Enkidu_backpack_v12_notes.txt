ENKIDU — BACKPACK / V12

Reference: the four-view backpack sketch supplied in this chat, exec-a2165755-2984-43f5-bf48-4672c7e39661.png. A packed copy is included in the Blender files.

DELIVERABLES
Enkidu_backpack_sketch_v12.blend — full character with rebuilt backpack, prior backpack preserved in a disabled collection, isolated backpack review scene and separate worn-fit review scene.
Enkidu_backpack_standalone_v12.blend — backpack, procedural materials, reference, studio lights and review cameras. The character is not included.

CONSTRUCTION
01. Soft tapered hollow canvas shell, approximately 22 cm wide, 12–13 cm deep and 31 cm high, scaled to the existing character. Rounded base and localized lower-face compression folds.
02. Separate rounded fold-over flap, rolled edge binding, individual edge stitches and short loose fibres. The FLAP CONTROL empty can rotate around its local X axis; approximately 120 degrees raises the flap for inspection. This is a rigid inspection control, not a cloth simulation.
03. Single rounded canvas closure tab and aged brass button. The closed outer view determines the fastening; the duplicated tabs in the sketch's open view are interpreted as one coherent closure.
04. Flat woven shoulder straps fitted around the character, with seam stitches placed against the evaluated fabric surface.
05. Rounded rectangular brass adjusters, center bars and separate webbing return pieces.
06. Reinforced lower anchor tabs with box-and-cross stitching.
07. Sewn side gussets with soft vertical creases.
08. Reinforced base strip, perimeter stitching, rubbed fabric tones and sparse fraying.
09. Bound compartment mouth with actual fabric thickness and darker inner lining.
10. Narrow folded webbing carry loop with stitched edges.

MATERIALS
Procedural brown canvas, warp/weft relief and yarn color variation, localized edge fading, dark lining, flax thread and worn brass. These materials render natively in Blender. They have not been baked into game-engine texture maps.

REVIEW
Checked isolated outer, body-side, side, open and detail renders and a separate fit render on the character. This is a detailed modeling interpretation of the sketch, not a scan. Fabric wear is restrained and does not reproduce every drawn mark. The existing head and body were retained. No character rig, cloth simulation, or game-ready retopology was added.

BACKUP
The incoming live scene, including its unsaved state, was saved before editing to character-3d/enkidu-fresh/Enkidu_before_backpack_v12.blend. The earlier v11 project was not overwritten.
