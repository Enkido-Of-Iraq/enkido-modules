ENKIDU - SKETCH-FITTED HEAD STUDY, V08

Current file: Enkidu_sketch_head_v08.blend
Shape reference: concept-art/turnarounds/enkidu-head-turnaround-v1.png
Colour reference: original exec-400a2b28-6e40-4b0b-98fc-ce9fdfb4f7ff.png
Both reference images are packed in the Blender file.

Changes
- Reconstructed face, skull, jaw and neck using the front and profile sketch.
- Sculpted blended nasal bridge, tip, alar wings, philtrum, lips and chin volumes.
- Inset spherical eyes, iris geometry and continuous eyelid tissue joined to head.
- Fine eyelashes, irregular eyebrow fibres and textured eye pigmentation.
- New continuous ear surfaces with helix, antihelix, concha, tragus and lobule.
- Editable strand groom with layered open S waves, short filler and flyaways.
- Real skin subsurface, pore bump, roughness and original-reference colour.

Viewing
The file opens with the front comparison camera and material preview.
Additional orthographic cameras: left-facing profile, right-facing profile,
and three-quarter review. Left/right labels refer to nose direction on the page.
The supplied sketch image is in its own viewport reference collection.
The previous V04 head is preserved in a hidden collection.
The pre-existing body and wardrobe remain in the file.

Status
This is an editable likeness study. It is not a scan or a final production asset.
Profile shape, eyelid thickness and hair flow remain approximate interpretations.
It is not rigged and does not yet have animation-ready facial retopology,
a facial expression system, or a complete independent UV skin texture set.
