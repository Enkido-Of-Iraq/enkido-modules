## Shared 60-second route

Use the same development-build sequence on desktop and mobile: 0–5 seconds idle; 5–15 walk forward; 15–25 walking turns; 25–40 run forward with alternating turns; 40–48 release and stop; 48–54 brake/reverse; 54–60 walk and camera orbit/recenter. Begin from the serialized `AnzuDesert` spawn and do not alter quality settings between comparison runs.
