# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Defines how Enkidu presents a difficult survival journey with emotional seriousness while remaining appropriate for a 7+ audience.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Non-graphic depiction of danger
The game SHALL portray danger through implication, player escape, protective adults, sound, framing, and environmental consequences. It MUST NOT graphically depict violence, injury, medical harm, torture, or harm to children.

#### Scenario: Player encounters a dangerous adult
- **WHEN** an encounter creates a threat to the boy
- **THEN** the game presents a readable escape or protection objective without showing graphic harm

### Requirement: Child-safe treatment of exploitation themes
Threats involving exploitation, kidnapping, trafficking, or medical danger SHALL be abstracted as unsafe situations the child notices and escapes. The game MUST NOT describe, show, or instruct on bodily harm or organ theft.

#### Scenario: Player recognizes an unsafe situation
- **WHEN** narrative cues indicate that an adult situation is unsafe
- **THEN** the player receives a clear non-graphic choice or route to seek help, hide, or leave

### Requirement: Violence-free player expression
The protagonist's primary responses to danger SHALL be hiding, fleeing, distracting, seeking help, crafting non-graphic survival aids, and solving environmental problems. The player MUST NOT be required to kill or graphically injure people to progress.

#### Scenario: Player faces a guarded route
- **WHEN** a guarded route blocks progress
- **THEN** the mission supplies a stealth, distraction, alternate route, or assistance-based solution

### Requirement: Recovery and care
After intense sequences, the story SHALL provide moments of safety, reflection, care, or hope. Failure feedback MUST be brief, non-graphic, and support an immediate safe retry.

#### Scenario: Player fails a stealth escape
- **WHEN** the player is discovered during an escape sequence
- **THEN** the game returns the player to a safe retry point with non-graphic feedback
