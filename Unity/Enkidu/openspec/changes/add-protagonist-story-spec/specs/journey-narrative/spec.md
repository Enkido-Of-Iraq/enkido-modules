# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Defines the chaptered father-search journey that carries the protagonist through war-affected Iraq and gives missions their emotional purpose.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Family motivation
The story SHALL establish that the boy is separated from his father and leaves his mother and six-month-old sister at home while seeking information that may help bring his father back safely. This motivation MUST remain understandable throughout the journey.

#### Scenario: Player begins a later chapter
- **WHEN** the story transitions to a new location
- **THEN** the chapter preserves or advances the player's understanding of the boy's search for his father and concern for his family

### Requirement: Chaptered Iraqi journey
The game SHALL structure the journey as authored chapters across Iraqi towns and cities affected by war. Travel, including old buses marked by the period's hardship, MUST be used as both a transition and a setting for story encounters.

#### Scenario: Player travels between locations
- **WHEN** a chapter requires travel to a new town or city
- **THEN** the game uses a story transition that communicates distance, changing conditions, and the continuation of the father's search

### Requirement: Varied human encounters
The journey SHALL include people who offer food, shelter, guidance, or clues, as well as people whose behavior requires the boy to observe, hide, decline, or escape. Encounters MUST give the player agency or meaningful information.

#### Scenario: Player meets a helpful traveler
- **WHEN** the boy encounters a person who offers help
- **THEN** the encounter provides a credible form of care, guidance, or a lead that can affect the next objective

### Requirement: Escalating clues
The story SHALL reveal information about the father's possible whereabouts through a chain of observable leads rather than a single unexplained reveal. Later leads MAY point to an underground political-prison facility.

#### Scenario: Player obtains a prison lead
- **WHEN** the player completes a relevant clue encounter
- **THEN** the mission journal identifies why the lead matters and what the next reachable destination is

### Requirement: Prison approach and survival chapter
The underground prison chapter SHALL focus on stealth, observation, navigation, evidence, and survival while the boy seeks information about his father. The chapter MUST portray the setting through implication and environmental storytelling rather than graphic depiction of prisoner abuse.

#### Scenario: Player enters the prison area
- **WHEN** the boy reaches the prison approach
- **THEN** the game establishes danger through atmosphere, restricted routes, and guarded spaces while preserving the player's clear escape and objective options
