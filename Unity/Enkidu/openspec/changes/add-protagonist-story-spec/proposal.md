# Proposal

## Why

Enkidu's main character is the emotional and playable center of the game, but his artwork direction, abilities, family motivation, and journey are not yet defined as a coherent contract. Establishing them now ensures that future Blender work, animation, missions, dialogue, and gameplay all tell the same story.

## What Changes

- Define the protagonist as a healthy, thin, agile, and observant 11-year-old Iraqi boy whose physical size supports fast travel and access to small hiding spaces.
- Establish a character-art direction that balances believable childhood vulnerability with resilience, curiosity, intelligence, and growing survival competence.
- Define his family motivation: he is separated from his father, leaves his mother and six-month-old sister at home, and undertakes a journey to find and return his father safely.
- Define a chaptered journey through war-affected Iraqi towns and cities, including old buses, strangers who help or threaten him, discovered leads, and an underground political-prison destination.
- Define encounters as age-appropriate suspense, care, observation, escape, and survival rather than graphic violence or explicit harm.
- Define how protagonist growth connects narrative experiences to future exploration, repair, crafting, stealth, and clue-finding gameplay.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `protagonist-character`: The main boy's artistic identity, physical profile, playable strengths, emotional expression, and growth.
- `journey-narrative`: The game’s chaptered father-search journey, family motivation, travel, encounters, clues, and prison destination.
- `age-appropriate-tension`: Narrative and gameplay boundaries for portraying danger and difficult themes to a 7+ audience.

### Modified Capabilities

- None.

## Impact

- Guides Blender character art, rigs, facial expressions, animation, Unity player configuration, interaction design, mission writing, dialogue, cinematics, environmental art, UI, audio, and accessibility.
- Will be implemented after the game-foundation runtime specification is approved and applied; no Unity scenes or gameplay code change in this planning-only proposal.
