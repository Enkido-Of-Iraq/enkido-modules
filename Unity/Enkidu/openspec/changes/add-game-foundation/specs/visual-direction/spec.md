# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Defines a performance-aware visual identity for Enkidu that evokes 1990s southern Iraq through color, texture, light, and atmosphere.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Setting readability
Each mission SHALL communicate its southern-Iraq setting through a deliberate combination of environmental palette, surface textures, lighting, architecture, vegetation, props, and ambient effects appropriate to its narrative location.

#### Scenario: Player enters a marshland mission
- **WHEN** the player enters a southern-Iraq marshland area
- **THEN** the environment conveys place and time through its visual composition rather than relying only on explanatory text

### Requirement: Stylized production scope
The visual style SHALL prioritize readable silhouettes, intentional materials, textures, color grading, lighting, and atmospheric effects over unnecessarily dense geometry or photorealistic asset complexity.

#### Scenario: Asset is prepared for a mission
- **WHEN** an environment asset is accepted for use in a mission
- **THEN** its visual contribution and performance cost are reviewed against the project's stylized production scope

### Requirement: Cross-platform visual quality
The game SHALL preserve the mission's essential palette, material identity, readability, and atmosphere on both desktop and mobile. Platform quality settings MAY reduce secondary detail but MUST not alter gameplay-relevant visibility.

#### Scenario: Mission runs on mobile quality settings
- **WHEN** a mission is played using mobile visual settings
- **THEN** interactable objects, hazards, paths, and hiding feedback remain readable
